Mohamed H. Mousa, Ph.D.

Neuroengineer & Research Scientist

I specialize in computational neuroscience, high-density electrophysiology, and machine learning , developing end-to-end pipelines that translate complex neural signals into meaningful insight. My work spans ALS non-invasive biomarker discovery, spinal network dynamics, motoneuron biophysics, and brain-computer interface research. By bridging biology and engineering, I am interested in building systems that deepen our understanding of the nervous system and advance technologies that restore and extend human function.
